FAQ

Before you buy.

Are ACS products accounting software?

No. They are spreadsheet-based control layers designed to work alongside the accounting or finance systems you already use.

Do I need advanced spreadsheet skills?

The products are designed for clear first-use operation, with blank and demo versions plus buyer documentation. Complex administration is kept out of the normal workflow where possible.

Are ACS products internationally usable?

The core systems are jurisdiction-neutral where stated. They do not replace country-specific tax, payroll, legal, statutory, lender, or accounting-policy advice.

Do spreadsheet protections equal security?

No. Range or worksheet protections are accidental-change controls. They are not enterprise security or database-grade permission systems.
